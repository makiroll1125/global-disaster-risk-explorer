Switch between records, deaths, affected populations and damage to compare different dimensions of disaster impacts.

What these numbers mean

A record is one disaster’s impact in one country. A disaster crossing borders appears more than once. Affected totals can include the same people across multiple disasters.

Blank impacts remain unknown, absent or unreported - not automatically zero. Coverage shows the share of records with a value. Damage is inflation-adjusted to 2025 US dollars; 2026 adjusted figures are unavailable.

This is a history of reported major disasters, not a forecast of risk or a comparison adjusted for population. Reporting differences affect comparisons and trends.

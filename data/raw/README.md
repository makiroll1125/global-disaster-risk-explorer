# Original EM-DAT export

Place the unmodified EM-DAT public-table XLSX or CSV here. Download through your own account at https://public.emdat.be/.
The preparation script also copies an export supplied from another local path into this directory, without changing its bytes.

The export dated 2026-09-15 is present and inspected: 10,896 records, natural disasters only, 2000–2026. Its SHA-256 and field inspection are saved in `data/processed/quality-report.json`. The source file is unchanged.
